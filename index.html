Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

Disclaimer: This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Road Safety Authority (RSA) of Ireland. Routes are independently created for practice purposes only. Always follow official RSA guidance and road laws.

This Privacy Policy describes how Irish Driving Apps ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Irish Driving Test Routes Android application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect any information that directly identifies you (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

The App does collect the following categories of data automatically:

  • Usage analytics – anonymous events such as which city or test centre you select, which route you view, whether the paywall is shown, and whether an in-app purchase is completed. This data is collected by Google Firebase Analytics and cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports – if the App crashes, an anonymous report including the device model, Android version, and a stack trace is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics. This report does not contain personal information.
  • Location data – the App requests access to your device's precise and approximate location only while the App is in use, solely to display your current position on the driving-test route map. Your location data is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. It stays on your device.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To display your position on the route map (location — on-device only).
  • To understand how users interact with the App so we can improve it (analytics).
  • To identify and fix software bugs (crash reports).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not listed above.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes. Data flows only to the following sub-processors as part of operating the App:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) – operated by Google LLC. See Firebase Privacy Information.
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google. We never see or store your payment details.

4. Map Data

The App displays maps using OpenStreetMap tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under ODbL). Map tile requests are sent to OpenStreetMap servers; no personal information is included in these requests.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all driving-test routes. All transactions are handled by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information.

6. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics retains aggregated event data for up to 14 months by default. Crashlytics reports are retained for 90 days. Neither service stores data in a form that could identify an individual user.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data held about you. Because we do not collect identifiable personal data, we cannot link analytics or crash data to a specific individual. However, if you have a question or request, please contact us using the details below.

You may also opt out of Firebase Analytics data collection by adjusting your device's ad-personalisation settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID on Android 12+).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
